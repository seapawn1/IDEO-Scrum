# Product log details

Three parts of the product log: starting from a blank page, what the seven item columns hold, and who decides the items. Read this file when you write a product log for a product whose goal is not yet clear (`blank-page`), when you add or change an item (`item-columns`), or when you decide the items (`items-with-sm`).

## Blank page (`blank-page`)

Most projects can state a Product statement and a first Product Goal directly. When you cannot yet write them, Empathize has not gone far enough: use the IDEO five modes (`../ideo-kernel/SKILL.md`), starting with Empathize (`../ideo-kernel/IDEO-modes/empathize.md`) and Define (`../ideo-kernel/IDEO-modes/define.md`), to understand who the users are, what they struggle with, and what value the product should deliver — then write the conclusions into `scrum/ProductBacklog.md`.

- Record one or two methods directly in the product log's Notes, or open an IDEO log (`scrum/ideo-<suffix>/IdeoBacklog.md`, `open-ideo-log`) if the discovery spans several methods or sessions.
- **Optional goal-stress check**: when framing the Product Goal or early product questions, also ask what failure assumptions could break the goal and turn each risky assumption into a concrete question to validate.
- Write into the product log whatever is already clear (such as the stakeholder table in §2.1), and turn remaining unknowns into Product Backlog Items (`no-tbd`) or IDEO discovery questions rather than leaving TBD placeholders.

## Seven columns (`item-columns`)

| Column | What goes in it |
| --- | --- |
| # | PBI- and three digits, counting up: PBI-001, PBI-002, and so on |
| Title | A short name |
| User story | "As <someone from section 2.1>, I want …, so that …" |
| Place | A step of the map, or a subsection of section 2.2 |
| Status | Where the item stands: waiting, in Sprint NN |
| Size | S — one or two sessions; M — three to five sessions; L — more than five sessions, to be split when refining |
| Notes | Source, open points, draft acceptance criteria, refinement notes |

## Items with the Scrum Master (`items-with-sm`)

- Let the Product Owner decide the items together with the Scrum Master, who serves the Product Owner; acts for them within a written handover, and the accountability stays with the Product Owner; decisions made under it are recorded.
- At Planning the Product Owner leads the selection, and the Scrum Master and the Developer say when it is too much or too little; the measure is the Sprint Goal, and the check when the rows are written is `select-items`.
- This departs from the Scrum Guide: see `details/departures.md`.
