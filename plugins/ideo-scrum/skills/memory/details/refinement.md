# Refinement of the selected items

How the items of a Sprint are refined, in the What stage. Read this file when you refine the items in a sprint log's section 2.2, before drafting a refinement even in chat. The list line that points here is `refinement`; the lines `select-items`, `refine-in-sprint`, `acceptance-written-once` and `where-refined-content` cover what goes before and around it.

## Who and what is written

Refine the items one at a time, led by the Scrum Master with the Product Owner; the Developer joins. Write into section 2.2 what was settled and who decided it, the acceptance criteria, and the tests.

## The seven steps

1. Take one item; it is the point of view.
2. Confirm the point of view, copied from the goal or the item without change.
3. Open the item into How Might We questions and unclear points.
4. Place each point: the Product Owner decides it, the Developer proposes a solution, or it needs research — a question about what people want or what counts as good, with no ready answer — which the Product Owner settles by the boundary in `sprint-or-modes`: a design sprint, or the five modes.
5. Cut the item into items of a few sessions at most, with acceptance criteria.
6. Check that no questions are left — the Developer's about what is meant, the Product Owner's about what will be built: the Developer restates each point, and the Product Owner agrees.
7. The Product Owner says when refinement is over.
