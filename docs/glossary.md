The plugin's own terms, in the plugin's own words, in English and Chinese. The methods behind them come from the Scrum Guide, the Scrum Guide Expansion Pack, the Stanford d.school and the book *Sprint*; their sources and licenses are in [ATTRIBUTION.md](../ATTRIBUTION.md). ## Logs and files 日志和文件

| Term | 中文 | Meaning | 含义 | See |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| product log | 产品日志 | The Product Backlog, kept in `scrum/ProductBacklog.md`: the product, the Product Goal, the Definition of Done, who the stakeholders are, one map, and the items. | 即 Product Backlog，放在 `scrum/ProductBacklog.md`：产品、Product Goal、完成的定义、利益相关者、一张图，还有条目。 | [product-log](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/scrum-kernel/conventions/product-log.md) |
| sprint log | 冲刺日志 | The Sprint Backlog, kept in `SprintBacklog.md` in the Sprint's folder: five chapters (Why, What, How, Increment notes, Test); the record of one Sprint. | 即 Sprint Backlog，放在冲刺文件夹里的 `SprintBacklog.md`：五章（Why、What、How、增量笔记、Test），是一个冲刺的记录。 | [sprint-log](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/sprint-kernel/conventions/sprint-log.md) |
| design log | 设计日志 | `DesignBacklog.md`: the record of one design, from the challenge to tested prototypes and conclusions. | `DesignBacklog.md`：一次设计的记录，从课题到测试过的原型和结论。 | [design-log](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/design-kernel/conventions/design-log.md) |
| IDEO log | IDEO 日志 | `IdeoBacklog.md`: the record of a run of the five modes that is more than one or two methods, or more than one session; five chapters, one per mode. | `IdeoBacklog.md`：一轮五模式的记录，用在不止一两个方法、或者不止一个会话的时候；五章，每个模式一章。 | [ideo-log](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/ideo-kernel/conventions/ideo-log.md), convention 3 |
| PBI | 产品日志条目 | Product Backlog Item: one row of the product log's item table, with a number, a title, a user story, a place on the map, a status, a size and notes. | Product Backlog Item：产品日志条目表里的一行，有编号、标题、用户故事、在图上的位置、状态、大小和备注。 | [product-log](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/scrum-kernel/conventions/product-log.md), convention 10 |
| Commitment | 承诺 | The one-row table that holds a log's fixed goal word for word: the Product Goal, the Sprint Goal or the design goal. Only the Product Owner changes it. | 日志里一行的小表，逐字写着这份日志不变的目标：Product Goal、Sprint Goal 或设计目标。只有 Product Owner 能改。 | [overview](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/scrum-kernel/overview.md), convention 17 |
| Now / Waiting for PO | Now / Waiting for PO（日志头部两行） | The two lines at the top of a Sprint, design or IDEO log: where the work stands, and which decisions wait for the Product Owner (or None). A new session reads them first. | 冲刺、设计、IDEO 日志头部的两行：工作到哪了，哪些决定在等 Product Owner（没有就写 None）。新会话先读它们。 | [sprint-log](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/sprint-kernel/conventions/sprint-log.md), convention 22 |
| Increment notes | 增量笔记 | Chapter 4 of the sprint log: the full record of what was done and decided, enough for a new session to pick the Sprint up from the log alone. | 冲刺日志的第 4 章：做了什么、定了什么的完整记录，够让新会话单凭日志接手这个冲刺。 | [sprint-log](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/sprint-kernel/conventions/sprint-log.md), convention 9 |
| role card | 角色卡 | One row of the product log's stakeholder table; a Stakeholder subagent takes it as its identity. | 产品日志里利益相关者表的一行；Stakeholder 子代理拿它当自己的身份。 | [product-log](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/scrum-kernel/conventions/product-log.md), convention 6 |
| the Map | 架构图 | Section 2.2 of the product log: one flowchart with the people on top, the ending at the bottom and the steps between; items are placed on its steps. | 产品日志的 2.2：一张流程图，人在上面，结局在下面，中间是步骤；条目挂在步骤上。 | [product-log](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/scrum-kernel/conventions/product-log.md), convention 7 |
| board | 看板 | `scrum/Board.html`: one page that shows where the project stands, written from the logs; it keeps no state of its own. | `scrum/Board.html`：一页看清项目走到哪，由日志写成，自己不存状态。 | [board](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/scrum-kernel/conventions/board.md) |
| `scrum/documents/` | 归档文件夹 | Where files that serve the whole project go, including the archived sprint, design and IDEO logs. | 为整个项目服务的文件放在这里，包括归档的冲刺日志、设计日志和 IDEO 日志。 | [overview](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/scrum-kernel/overview.md), convention 5 |

## Roles and handoffs 角色和分工

| Term | 中文 | Meaning | 含义 | See |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product Owner | Product Owner（PO） | The one role that must be a person. Decides the value and direction, the goals, which items enter a Sprint, the go-ahead, and whether to move on at the end of each stage. | 唯一必须是人的角色。定价值和方向、各个目标、哪些条目进冲刺、放行，以及每个阶段结束时往不往下走。 | [overview](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/scrum-kernel/overview.md), conventions 8 and 9 |
| Scrum Master | Scrum Master | The AI role that facilitates Planning, checkpoints, Review and Retro, reviews the work, and serves the Product Owner. | 主持 Planning、检查点、Review 和 Retro，审阅工作，为 Product Owner 服务的 AI 角色。 | [overview](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/scrum-kernel/overview.md), convention 8 |
| Developer | Developer | The role that writes the plan (the How), builds the Increment and keeps the sprint log. | 写计划（How）、做增量、记冲刺日志的角色。 | [overview](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/scrum-kernel/overview.md), convention 8 |
| Designer | Designer | The design pairing, a role of the plugin's own that Scrum does not have: research and prototypes, with conclusions that go back to the product log. | 设计结对，是插件自己的角色，Scrum 里没有：做研究和原型，结论回到产品日志。 | [overview](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/scrum-kernel/overview.md), convention 8 |
| Stakeholder | Stakeholder | An outside view of the product, given by a fresh subagent that takes a role card; or a standing session that does chores. | 对产品的外部视角：由一个拿着角色卡的全新子代理给出；或者是一个常驻做杂务的会话。 | [product-log](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/scrum-kernel/conventions/product-log.md), convention 6 |
| PO assistant | PO 助手 | A standing Stakeholder session for chores that touch no source: organizing logs, summarizing progress, maintaining the board. It does not rank, decide or review. | 常驻的 Stakeholder 会话，做不碰源码的杂务：整理日志、汇总进度、维护看板。它不排序、不拍板、不审阅。 | [overview](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/scrum-kernel/overview.md), convention 14 |
| fresh subagent | 全新子代理 | A subagent that starts from its brief alone; a fork or a copy of a session does not count. | 只凭一份简报起步的子代理；会话的分身或副本不算。 | [overview](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/scrum-kernel/overview.md), convention 7 |
| written handover | 书面授权 | Some of the Product Owner's decisions handed to the Scrum Master in writing, in the log; the accountability stays with the Product Owner. | Product Owner 把自己的一部分决定书面交给 Scrum Master，写在日志里；责任仍在 Product Owner。 | [overview](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/scrum-kernel/overview.md), convention 12 |
| go-ahead | 放行 | The Product Owner's word that building may start; approving a plan is not one. The log records how far it reaches: commit, merge, tag, release, push. | Product Owner 说可以动手了；认可计划不算。日志里写明它放行到哪一步：提交、合并、打 tag、发版、推送。 | [sprint-log](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/sprint-kernel/conventions/sprint-log.md), convention 8 |
| checkpoint | 检查点 | A stop to check the work against the Sprint Goal: at the end of each stage, whenever the AI is stuck, and whenever the Product Owner asks how things stand. It replaces the Daily Scrum. | 对照 Sprint Goal 检查工作的一次停顿：每个阶段结束、AI 卡住、Product Owner 问进展的时候。它代替 Daily Scrum。 | [sprint-log](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/sprint-kernel/conventions/sprint-log.md), convention 10 |

## Process 流程

| Term | 中文 | Meaning | 含义 | See |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| the five stages of a Sprint | Sprint 的五个阶段 | Why, What, How, Increment, Test. The Product Owner decides at the end of each. | Why、What、How、Increment、Test。每个阶段结束，由 Product Owner 定。 | [sprint-log](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/sprint-kernel/conventions/sprint-log.md), convention 24 |
| refinement | 精化 | Working an item out until no questions are left: seven steps, one item at a time, led by the Scrum Master with the Product Owner, finished in section 2.2 of the sprint log. | 把一条条目弄到没有疑问：七步，一次一条，由 Scrum Master 和 Product Owner 带着做，在冲刺日志的 2.2 里收尾。 | [sprint-log](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/sprint-kernel/conventions/sprint-log.md), convention 6 |
| Review / Retro / closing commit | 收官：Review、Retro 与收官提交 | The close of a Sprint. Review compresses and archives the logs and refreshes the product log; Retro writes the lessons into the project's memory or instructions; one commit holds both, is tagged with the Sprint folder's name and merged. | 冲刺的收尾。Review 压缩、归档日志并刷新产品日志；Retro 把教训写进项目的记忆或指令文件；一个提交装下两者，打上冲刺文件夹的名字作 tag，再合并。 | [sprint-log](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/sprint-kernel/conventions/sprint-log.md), conventions 17 to 19 |
| small Sprint | 小冲刺 | Work of half an hour to one session still gets a Sprint, with each step shrunk to a line or two. | 半小时到一个会话的活也开冲刺，每一步缩成一两行。 | [sprint-log](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/sprint-kernel/conventions/sprint-log.md), convention 3 |
| kernel | 内核 | One of the plugin's four skills: ideo, scrum, sprint, design. Each holds conventions and source material, loaded when needed. | 插件的四个技能之一：ideo、scrum、sprint、design。各自装着约定和原始材料，用到才加载。 | [overview](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/scrum-kernel/overview.md), convention 8 |
| commitments hook | 承诺 hook | A hook that puts the commitments written in the logs into the session's context at start, resume, clear and compaction, and again when they change. Switch it off with `IDEO_SCRUM_COMMITMENTS=off`. | 一个 hook：会话开始、恢复、清空、压缩时，把日志里写的承诺放进上下文，承诺变了再放一次。用 `IDEO_SCRUM_COMMITMENTS=off` 关掉。 | [hooks.json](../plugins/ideo-scrum/hooks/hooks.json) |
| Definition of Outcome Done / Output Done | 结果完成的定义 / 产出完成的定义 | The two halves of the Definition of Done in the product log. Outcome Done is what shows the product delivers value to its stakeholders; Output Done is the quality standard every Increment meets. | 产品日志里完成的定义的两半。结果完成，是产品给利益相关者带来价值的证据；产出完成，是每个增量都要达到的质量标准。 | [product-log](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/scrum-kernel/conventions/product-log.md), convention 5 |
| design sprint | 设计冲刺 | A heavy research module beside the Sprint: five stages, each standing on one of the five modes, with the book's steps, a Product Owner decision between stages, and a prototype tested with role cards. Not the same thing as the five modes. | 与冲刺并列的一个重型研究模块：五个阶段，各站在五模式中的一个上，带着书里的步骤、阶段之间的 Product Owner 决定，和一个用角色卡测试的原型。和五模式不是一回事。 | [design-log](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/design-kernel/conventions/design-log.md), [ideo-log](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/ideo-kernel/conventions/ideo-log.md), convention 2 |
| the five modes | 五模式 | IDEO's Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype and Test, with a library of methods. They are modes, not stages: no fixed order, and you can go back. What users call "the IDEO five-stage method" is these. Not the same thing as the design sprint. | IDEO 的 Empathize、Define、Ideate、Prototype、Test，配一套方法库。它们是模式，不是阶段：没有固定顺序，可以回头。用户说的「IDEO 五阶段法」就是它们。和设计冲刺不是一回事。 | [ideo-log](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/ideo-kernel/conventions/ideo-log.md), convention 2 |

## From d.school and the book *Sprint* 来自 d.school 和《Sprint》

| Term | 中文 | Meaning | 含义 | See |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Empathize | 共情 | The mode of understanding people: observe and listen to learn what they need. | 理解人的模式：观察、倾听，弄清他们要什么。 | [empathize](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/ideo-kernel/IDEO-modes/empathize.md) |
| Define | 定义 | The mode of framing the right problem from what you learned. | 把学到的东西收成一个对的问题的模式。 | [define](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/ideo-kernel/IDEO-modes/define.md) |
| Ideate | 构思 | The mode of opening up many possible answers before choosing one. | 先打开很多可能的答案，再选的模式。 | [ideate](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/ideo-kernel/IDEO-modes/ideate.md) |
| Prototype | 原型 | The mode of making an idea tangible, cheaply and roughly, to learn from it. | 把想法做成摸得着的东西，便宜、粗糙，为的是从中学到东西。 | [prototype](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/ideo-kernel/IDEO-modes/prototype.md) |
| Test | 测试 | The mode of putting a prototype in front of people to see what they do. Not the same as the Sprint's last stage, which is also called Test. | 把原型放到人面前，看他们怎么做的模式。和冲刺的最后一个阶段不是一回事，那个阶段也叫 Test。 | [test](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/ideo-kernel/IDEO-modes/test.md) |
| Point of View | 观点陈述 | One sentence that frames a user, a need and an insight, grounded in what you learned from people. | 一句话，框出一个用户、一个需要和一个洞察，依据是你从人那里学到的东西。 | [point-of-view](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/ideo-kernel/methods/point-of-view.md) |
| How Might We | 我们如何能 | A question that turns a problem into an invitation for ideas: "How might we ...?" | 把问题变成邀请的一种问法：「我们如何能……？」 | [how-might-we-questions](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/ideo-kernel/methods/how-might-we-questions.md) |
| Five-Act Interview | 五幕访谈 | The five-part shape of a test interview: a friendly welcome, general questions about the person, an introduction to the prototype, tasks that get them reacting to it, and a short debrief. | 测试访谈的五段式：友好的开场，问问这个人的一般情况，介绍原型，用任务让他对原型做出反应，最后简短复盘。 | [5-2-interview](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/design-kernel/steps/5-2-interview.md) |
| rumble | 对决 | When two good ideas conflict and cannot share one prototype, build both and test them head to head instead of choosing first. | 两个好想法打架、放不进同一个原型时，两个都做出来，面对面测，而不是先选一个。 | [3-4-rumble](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/design-kernel/steps/3-4-rumble.md) |
| Lightning Demos | 闪电演示 | Each person gives a three-minute tour of a solution they like, from other products, other fields or their own company, and the team collects the ideas worth borrowing. | 每个人用三分钟介绍一个自己喜欢的解法，来自别的产品、别的领域或自己的公司，团队把值得借的想法收集起来。 | [3-1-remix-and-improve](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/design-kernel/steps/3-1-remix-and-improve.md) |
| Make a Map | 画地图 | Drawing a simple diagram of how a customer moves through a product or service, from first contact to the outcome. The product log's Map is drawn this way. | 画一张简单的图，讲顾客怎么走过一个产品或服务，从第一次接触到结局。产品日志里的 Map 就是这样画的。 | [2-1-make-a-map](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/design-kernel/steps/2-1-make-a-map.md) |
