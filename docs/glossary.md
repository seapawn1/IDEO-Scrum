## Logs and files 日志和文件

| Term | 中文 | Meaning | 含义 | See |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| product log | 产品日志 | The Product Backlog, kept in `scrum/ProductBacklog.md`: the product, the Product Goal, the Definition of Done, who the stakeholders are, one map, and the items. | 即 Product Backlog，放在 `scrum/ProductBacklog.md`：产品、Product Goal、完成的定义、利益相关者、一张图，还有条目。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `one-product-log`, `write-product-log` |
| sprint log | 冲刺日志 | The Sprint Backlog, kept in `SprintBacklog.md` in the Sprint's folder: five chapters (Why, What, How, Increment notes, Test); the record of one Sprint. | 即 Sprint Backlog，放在冲刺文件夹里的 `SprintBacklog.md`：五章（Why、What、How、增量笔记、Test），是一个冲刺的记录。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `sprint-folder`, `templates-record-outputs` |
| IDEO log | IDEO 日志 | `IdeoBacklog.md`: the record of a run of the five modes that is more than one or two methods, or more than one session; five chapters, one per mode. | `IdeoBacklog.md`：一轮五模式的记录，用在不止一两个方法、或者不止一个会话的时候；五章，每个模式一章。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `open-ideo-log` |
| PBI | 产品日志条目 | Product Backlog Item: one row of the product log's item table, with a number, a title, a user story, a place on the map, a status, a size and notes. | Product Backlog Item：产品日志条目表里的一行，有编号、标题、用户故事、在图上的位置、状态、大小和备注。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `item-columns` |
| Commitment | 承诺 | The one-row table that holds a log's fixed commitment word for word: the product statement, the Product Goal or the Sprint Goal. Only the Product Owner changes it. Reasons below it are optional and separate; a stage decision keeps a historical snapshot, not another current commitment. | 日志里一行的小表，逐字写着这份日志不变的承诺：产品陈述、Product Goal 或 Sprint Goal。只有 Product Owner 能改。表下解释可选，不属于承诺；阶段决定行保留当时快照，不是另一份当前承诺。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `goal-fixed`, `commitment-table` |
| Now / Waiting for PO | Now / Waiting for PO（日志头部两行） | The two lines at the top of a Sprint or IDEO log: where the work stands, and which decisions wait for the Product Owner (or None). A new session reads them first. | 冲刺或 IDEO 日志头部的两行：工作到哪了，哪些决定在等 Product Owner（没有就写 None）。新会话先读它们。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `log-is-the-handoff` |
| Increment notes | 增量笔记 | Chapter 4 of the sprint log: the full record of what was done and decided, enough for a new session to pick the Sprint up from the log alone. | 冲刺日志的第 4 章：做了什么、定了什么的完整记录，够让新会话单凭日志接手这个冲刺。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `increment-notes`, `mark-done` |
| role card | 角色卡 | One row of the product log's stakeholder table; a Stakeholder subagent takes it as its identity. | 产品日志里利益相关者表的一行；Stakeholder 子代理拿它当自己的身份。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `stakeholder-table` |
| the Map | 架构图 | Section 2.2 of the product log: one top-down Mermaid flowchart showing how actors move through the product to its ending, with five to fifteen steps between; items are placed on its steps. | 产品日志的 2.2：一张自上而下的 Mermaid 流程图，标出参与者经过产品走到结局的 5–15 个主步骤；条目挂在步骤上。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `architecture-map` |
| board | 看板 | `scrum/ScrumBoard.html`: one page showing the product and open Scrum Sprints, written from the logs; it keeps no state of its own and does not cover standalone IDEO logs. | `scrum/ScrumBoard.html`：一页看清产品和开着的 Scrum 冲刺，由日志写成，自己不存状态，不覆盖独立 IDEO 日志。 | [board](../plugins/ideo-scrum/board/board-log.md) |
| `scrum/documents/` | 归档文件夹 | Where files that serve the whole project go, including archived sprint logs and archived IDEO logs (`scrum/documents/ideo/`). | 为整个项目服务的文件放在这里，包括归档的冲刺日志和归档的 IDEO 日志（`scrum/documents/ideo/`）。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `where-files-go` |

## Roles and handoffs 角色和分工

| Term | 中文 | Meaning | 含义 | See |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product Owner | Product Owner（PO） | The one role that must be a person. Decides the value and direction, the goals, which items enter a Sprint, the go-ahead, and whether a Sprint stage may start or be returned to. | 唯一必须是人的角色。定价值和方向、各个目标、哪些条目进冲刺、放行，以及能否开始或返回冲刺的阶段。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `role-entry`, `only-po-human` |
| Scrum Master | Scrum Master | The AI role that facilitates Planning, checkpoints, Review and Retro, pairs with the Product Owner on IDEO Empathize and Test, reviews the work, and serves the Product Owner. | 主持 Planning、检查点、Review 和 Retro，陪 Product Owner 做 IDEO 共情与测试，审阅工作，为 Product Owner 服务的 AI 角色。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `role-entry` |
| Developer | Developer | The role that writes the solution plan (the How), builds the Increment, keeps the sprint log, and handles IDEO Ideate, Prototype (kept out of Increments) and Test. | 写方案计划（How）、做增量、记冲刺日志，并承接 IDEO 构思、原型（不进增量）与测试的角色。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `role-entry` |
| Stakeholder | Stakeholder | An outside view of the product, given by a fresh subagent that takes a role card. | 对产品的外部视角：由一个拿着角色卡的全新子代理给出。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `stakeholder-table` |
| Supporter | 秘书 | The role for standing chores that touch no source: keeping the board, organizing the product log, summarizing progress, relaying what the Product Owner or Scrum Master already decided, explaining the mechanism. It is also the Product Owner's contact point: it gathers the questions waiting for them, sends back those that are not theirs, and carries their answers back word for word. What it relays marked as the Product Owner's own words, with the date, counts as theirs. It does not rank, decide, answer for the Product Owner or review. In the Scrum Guide Expansion Pack a Supporter is a Stakeholder type that does what is requested; the plugin takes only that side. | 做不碰源码的常驻杂活的角色：看板、整理产品日志、汇总进度、传 Product Owner 或 Scrum Master 已定的事、讲解机制。它也是 Product Owner 的联络点：收齐等他定的问题，把不归他的退回，把他的回答原话转回去。它转达时标明是 Product Owner 原话并附日期的，算 Product Owner 自己的意见。它不排序、不拍板、不替 Product Owner 回答、不审阅。SGEP 里的 Supporter 是 Stakeholder 的一种，照请求做事；插件只取这一面。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `who-gets-which-work`, `answers-back`, `po-word-relayed`, `relay-limits` |
| fresh subagent | 全新子代理 | A subagent that starts from its brief alone; a fork or a copy of a session does not count. | 只凭一份简报起步的子代理；会话的分身或副本不算。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `names`, `fresh-subagent` |
| written handover | 书面授权 | Some of the Product Owner's decisions handed to the Scrum Master in writing, in the log; the accountability stays with the Product Owner. Only decisions expressly handed over are covered; reviewing is not permission to move on. | Product Owner 把自己的一部分决定书面交给 Scrum Master，写在日志里；责任仍在 Product Owner。只在明授的决定范围内有效；审阅不是下一阶段放行。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `stage-gates` |
| go-ahead | 放行 | The Product Owner's word that building may start; approving a plan is not one. The log records how far it reaches: commit, merge, tag, release, push. | Product Owner 说可以动手了；认可计划不算。日志里写明它放行到哪一步：提交、合并、打 tag、发版、推送。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `go-ahead`, `review-handover` |
| checkpoint | 检查点 | A stop to check the work against the Sprint Goal: at the end of each stage, whenever the AI is stuck, and whenever the Product Owner asks how things stand. It replaces the Daily Scrum. | 对照 Sprint Goal 检查工作的一次停顿：每个阶段结束、AI 卡住、Product Owner 问进展的时候。它代替 Daily Scrum。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `checkpoints` |

## Process 流程

| Term | 中文 | Meaning | 含义 | See |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| the five stages of a Sprint | Sprint 的五个阶段 | Why, What, How, Increment, Test. The Product Owner settles each result and authorizes entry or return. | Why、What、How、Increment、Test。阶段结果由 Product Owner 定，进入或返回阶段也等明确放行。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `stage-gates`, `read-the-goal` |
| refinement | 精化 | Working an item out until no questions are left: seven steps, one item at a time, led by the Scrum Master with the Product Owner, finished in section 2.2 of the sprint log. | 把一条条目弄到没有疑问：七步，一次一条，由 Scrum Master 和 Product Owner 带着做，在冲刺日志的 2.2 里收尾。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `refinement` |
| Review / Retro / closing commit | 收官：Review、Retro 与收官提交 | The close of a Sprint. Review compresses and archives logs and refreshes the product log. Retro saves only important lessons as separately findable entries in project memory, or repository lesson notes (still one separately findable entry per lesson) if the tool has none; no important lesson means no entry, never a Sprint Retro summary or instructions-file entry. One closing commit holds only the actual in-repository changes, not copies of outside memory or invented Retro files; it is tagged with the Sprint folder’s name and merged. | 冲刺的收尾。Review 压缩并归档日志、刷新产品日志。Retro 只把重要教训分条存入项目记忆，工具没有项目记忆时用仓库里逐条可找回的教训笔记；没有重要教训就不写，不写每期 Retro 汇总或指令文件。一个收官提交只装实际仓内改动，不复制仓外记忆或凑 Retro 文件，打上冲刺文件夹的名字作 tag，再合并。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `review-at-close`, `disputed-points`, `retro-in-memory`, `one-closing-commit`, `outward-needs-po` |
| small Sprint | 小冲刺 | Work of half an hour to one session still gets a Sprint, with each step shrunk to a line or two. | 半小时到一个会话的活也开冲刺，每一步缩成一两行。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `small-sprint` |
| kernel | 内核 | One of the plugin's three skills holding methods and source material: ideo, scrum, sprint. The rules every role follows are in a fourth skill, the Memory. Claude Code role commands call their kernels in order, then the Memory; DSH role entries preload ideo, scrum and the Memory, then load sprint as needed. | 插件的三个技能之一：ideo、scrum、sprint，各自装着方法和原文。各角色共用的规矩在第四个技能 Memory 里。Claude Code 角色命令依次调用该角色的内核，再调用 Memory；DSH 的角色入口预载 ideo、scrum 和 Memory，sprint 按需加载。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `role-entry` |
| Memory | Memory | The plugin's one short list of working rules, a line each, with a details file behind the longer ones. Every role command loads it after the kernels, so the same rules hold without being told each time; where a rule does not fit, depart from it and say why in the log. | 插件的那份短规矩清单：一条一行，长的另有细则文件。每个角色命令在内核之后加载它，同一套规矩不用每次手动说明就能守；某条不合用时可以不照做，在日志里写明原因。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) |
| commitments hook | 承诺 hook | In Claude Code, a hook that puts the logs' commitments into context at start, resume, clear and compaction, and again when they change. In DSH, a model-step handler reads the logs and adds a snapshot when needed, without a shell hook. Switch off either with `IDEO_SCRUM_COMMITMENTS=off`. | 在 Claude Code 中，会话开始、恢复、清空、压缩或承诺变化时，hook 把日志里的承诺放进上下文。在 DSH 中，在模型执行前读取日志、按需补入承诺快照，不用 shell hook。两边均可用 `IDEO_SCRUM_COMMITMENTS=off` 关闭。 | [Board and hook](../README.md#board-and-hook) |
| Definition of Outcome Done / Output Done | 结果完成的定义 / 产出完成的定义 | The two halves of the Definition of Done in the product log. Outcome Done is what shows the product delivers value to its stakeholders; Output Done is the quality standard every Increment meets. Choosing verification priorities does not waive any standard. | 产品日志里完成的定义的两半。结果完成，是产品给利益相关者带来价值的证据；产出完成，是每个增量都要达到的质量标准。挑本期核验重点不等于豁免任何标准。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `dod-in-product-log`, `dod-standards` |
| the five modes | 五模式 | IDEO's Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype and Test, with a library of methods. They are modes, not rigid stages: no fixed order, you can go back and forth, and they form the shared toolbox behind every Scrum Sprint. What users call "the IDEO five-stage method" is these. | IDEO 的 Empathize、Define、Ideate、Prototype、Test，配一套方法库。它们是模式，不是死板阶段：没有固定顺序，可以来回切，是每个 Scrum 冲刺背后的共享工具箱。用户说的「IDEO 五阶段法」就是它们。 | [Memory](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/memory/SKILL.md) `open-ideo-log` |

| How Might We | 我们如何能 | A question that turns a problem into an invitation for ideas: "How might we ...?" | 把问题变成邀请的一种问法：「我们如何能……？」 | [how-might-we-questions](../plugins/ideo-scrum/skills/ideo-kernel/methods/how-might-we-questions.md) |
